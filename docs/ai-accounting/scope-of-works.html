The Build Plan

Three phases, each one builds on the last

Each phase is agreed, delivered, and signed off before the next begins. Payment is made at the start of each phase.

1

Bridgit Does the Bookkeeping Job

Bank statements and documents in. Working file out. Ready for your review.

5 Weeks
🎯

What Bridgit delivers: Your team provides the client's bank statement and any documents. Bridgit reads everything, categorises every transaction using MA's own account codes and VAT rules, cross-references invoices where available, builds the trial balance, runs the bank reconciliation, and produces MA's working Excel file and a one-page PDF summary.

What Bridgit does in Phase 1

  • Reads every transaction in the bank statement and categorises it using MA's account codes and VAT rules
  • Cross-references invoices and supporting documents provided with corresponding bank lines
  • Builds the trial balance and runs the bank reconciliation as hard software checks. If they do not agree, the job does not move forward
  • Produces MA's Excel working file in the exact format your team already uses, plus a one-page PDF summary
  • Flags uncertain transactions with a specific question. For example: "Emirates flight on 14 Jan. This client runs a car wash. Business trip or personal?" Never a silent guess
  • Saves every answer as a permanent rule for that client. The same question is never asked again
  • Logs the reason behind every coding decision. Every line is explainable and auditable
Why Bridgit beats a general AI tool

Bridgit is not given a blank prompt and a bank statement. It works from a knowledge base built from MA's own completed files, chart of accounts, VAT rules, and client history. It retrieves the most relevant past decisions for each new transaction before making a judgement. The more MA Associates provides, the better the output from day one.

Bridgit learns from every approved job

When your team approves a finished job, that entire output is permanently saved into Bridgit's knowledge base for that client. Every transaction, every account code, every decision. The next time a similar job comes in, Bridgit already knows how your team handled it. A general AI tool resets every session. Bridgit compounds. The value grows with every job completed.

2

Bridgit Reads the Evidence, Not Just the Statement

Invoice matched, verified, and logged. Missing documents surfaced before you ask.

5 Weeks · After Phase 1

In Phase 1, Bridgit reads the bank line and decides what account it belongs to. In Phase 2, it reads the invoice behind that line, verifies the net and VAT figures, and matches them together. The working file now shows whether each transaction has a confirmed invoice or whether one is missing and needs to be chased.

  • Deeper document processing — Bridgit reads every document in the client folder, cross-references invoices against bank lines, and builds an increasingly complete picture of each client's financial activity
  • FreeAgent connection — for clients already using FreeAgent, Bridgit pulls invoices directly without manual uploading. Read-only. No submissions via this route
  • UK tax research via Nylon — MA already subscribes to Nylon, a UK tax and HMRC research platform. For genuinely uncertain transactions, Bridgit consults it before deciding whether to flag a question. Fewer questions, better framed
  • Learning that compounds — by Phase 2, regular clients are processed noticeably faster because Bridgit has built up a real history of their approved work
  • Proactive deadline alerts — Bridgit monitors approaching VAT deadlines, year-end filing dates, and jobs waiting on missing documents. It alerts your team before anyone has to ask
  • Richer output — the working file includes prior year figures alongside current year, so comparatives are visible without pulling up last year's file separately
Note on scope flexibility

Some Phase 2 items, including the FreeAgent connection and deeper document processing, may be pulled forward into Phase 1 if we are ahead of schedule. Nothing is delayed for the sake of it.

3

Bridgit Is in the Room

Full accounts pipeline. WhatsApp presence. Workflow awareness.

After Phase 2

Phase 3 is where Bridgit becomes a team member, not just a tool. It is present in your WhatsApp group, aware of who is working on what, and handles the full accounts pipeline from source documents through to filed returns.

Full accounts pipeline

  • VAT return prepared by Bridgit, ready for review and sign-off
  • Personal tax returns populated from each client's income sources
  • Year-end accounts drafted with supporting schedules
  • Making Tax Digital (MTD) submission pathway, tested and working
  • All statutory submissions remain human-approved

WhatsApp and Meta integration

  • Bridgit joins your WhatsApp group and sends a daily briefing: jobs due this week, what is waiting on a decision, what is ready for review
  • Ask Bridgit in WhatsApp: "What's the status on ABC Ltd?" or "When is XYZ's VAT due?" Answered in the chat, no need to open a system
  • Bridgit listens to the group, picks up mentions of clients and jobs, and updates the relevant records automatically
  • Future option: Bridgit in a client-facing WhatsApp group, answering basic client queries with MA's oversight

Workflow awareness

  • Bridgit knows who is working on what job and at what stage
  • When a Bridgit job completes, it links automatically to the WIP board. No double entry
  • "Who's working on the ABC Ltd year-end?" answered instantly in WhatsApp or the dashboard

Specialist agents and per-client portal

  • Specialist AI agents for VAT, personal tax, and accounts preparation, all coordinated by Bridgit
  • Per-client portal: every job Bridgit has run for a client, current year status, upcoming deadlines, outstanding document requests
  • Foundation for a client-facing login if MA Associates chooses to offer that in the future

What Never Changes

The guarantees across all three phases

👤

Human approval on everything

Bridgit does not file anything, submit anything, or represent anything to HMRC or Companies House without explicit human authorisation.

🚫

No silent guesses

Every uncertain transaction either gets a confirmed coding with a logged reason, or comes back with a specific question. Never a silent wrong answer.

🔍

Full audit trail

Every decision Bridgit makes is logged. Click any transaction and see exactly why it was coded that way, which rule fired, and what was proposed.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Bridgit operates within the MA Associates database. AI providers are business-tier with zero data retention. Client data is not used to train any external model.
