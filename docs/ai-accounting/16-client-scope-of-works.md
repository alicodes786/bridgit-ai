## What We Are Building

**Bridgit** is an AI accounting assistant built specifically for MA Associates. It
takes the routine, time-consuming parts of bookkeeping and accounts work — the parts
that follow a process — and does them automatically, so your team spends their time
on the work that actually needs a qualified accountant.

Bridgit lives inside the MA Associates system your team already uses. There is no
new software to learn, no separate login, and no switching between tools. It works in
the background, comes back to your team only when it genuinely needs a human decision,
and produces output in the exact format your team already uses.

Bridgit is not a generic AI tool. It is built on MA Associates' own rules, chart of
accounts, prior client history, and completed working files — giving it context that
no off-the-shelf tool has. The output it produces is better than what any general AI
assistant could provide, because it knows your clients, your codes, and your process.

The build happens in three phases. Each phase delivers something real and usable
before the next one begins.

## Phase 1 — Bridgit Does the Bookkeeping Job
### Outcome: Documents in. Working file out. Ready for your review.

**What Bridgit delivers at the end of Phase 1:**

Your team uploads a client's bank statement and any supporting documents — invoices,
receipts, prior year files. Bridgit reads everything, builds its understanding of
that client from the documents provided, categorises every bank transaction using
MA's own account codes and VAT rules, cross-references invoices where available,
builds the trial balance, runs the bank reconciliation, and produces two files:

1. **MA's working Excel file** — in the exact format your team already uses, with all
   tabs and figures in the right place, ready for review
2. **A one-page summary PDF** — total transactions processed, how many were
   categorised automatically, how many came back with a question, trial balance
   status, bank rec status

**Why Bridgit's output is better than using a general AI tool:**

Bridgit is not given a blank prompt and a bank statement. It works from a
knowledge base built from your own completed files, your chart of accounts, your
VAT rules, and your clients' prior year history. It retrieves the most relevant past
decisions for each transaction before it makes a judgement. The more documents MA
Associates provides, the better the output — every invoice, every completed job, every
correction your team makes becomes part of Bridgit's permanent knowledge for that
client.

**Bridgit learns from every approved job:**

When your team reviews and approves a finished job, that entire output — every
transaction, every account code, every decision — is permanently saved into Bridgit's
knowledge base for that client. The next time a similar job comes in, Bridgit already
knows how your team handled it. It does not start from scratch. It learns from the
approved work and gets faster and more accurate with every job it completes. A general
AI tool resets every session. Bridgit compounds.

**What happens when Bridgit is not sure:**

Bridgit does not guess. It puts the line in an exceptions table with a specific
question for your team to answer — for example: "Emirates flight on 14 Jan — this
client runs a car wash. Business trip or personal?" Your answer is saved so Bridgit
does not ask the same question again for that client.

The trial balance and bank reconciliation are checked by software before the job
reaches your team. If they do not agree, the job does not move forward. Your team is
never asked to re-check basic arithmetic as part of the review.

**What your team does in Phase 1:**

- Start a job by typing in the Bridgit chat ("Start year-end for ABC Ltd") or
  uploading the files directly
- If you are on the Bridgit page when it needs a decision, the question appears in
  front of you immediately. If you are not, you get an email so nothing is missed
- Answer the exception questions in a simple table — one click per line
- Download the finished Excel and PDF, review, and approve

**Bridgit AI tab inside your dashboard:**

Bridgit appears as a tab in the MA Associates system. Every job shows a status:
Queued, Processing, Needs your input, Ready for review, or Approved. You always know
exactly where a job is without having to ask.

## Phase 2 — Bridgit Reads the Evidence, Not Just the Statement
### Outcome: Invoice matched, verified, and logged. Missing documents surfaced before you ask.

**What changes in Phase 2:**

In Phase 1, Bridgit reads the bank line and decides what account it belongs to. In
Phase 2, it goes further: it reads the invoice behind that bank line, verifies the
net and VAT figures, matches them together, and flags anything that does not add up.

The working file your team receives now shows, for each transaction: the account code,
the VAT treatment, and whether the invoice has been found and verified — or whether
one is missing and needs to be chased.

**What Phase 2 delivers:**

- **Deeper document processing** — Bridgit reads every document in the client folder,
  cross-references invoices against bank lines, and builds an increasingly complete
  picture of each client's financial activity
- **FreeAgent connection** — for clients already using FreeAgent, Bridgit pulls their
  invoices directly without your team needing to upload them manually (read-only — no
  submissions via this route)
- **UK tax research (Nylon)** — for complex or unusual transactions, Bridgit consults
  the UK tax and HMRC case database MA already subscribes to before deciding whether to
  flag a question. Fewer questions, better-framed ones
- **Learning that compounds** — every answer your team gives becomes a standing rule
  for that client. By Phase 2, regular clients are processed significantly faster
  because Bridgit already knows their patterns
- **Proactive alerts** — Bridgit monitors approaching VAT deadlines, year-end filing
  dates, and jobs waiting on missing documents. It sends an alert before anyone has to
  ask
- **Richer output** — the working file now includes prior year figures alongside
  current year, so your team can see comparatives without pulling up last year's file
  separately

**Timeline: 6–10 weeks after Phase 1 sign-off**

## Phase 3 — Bridgit Is in the Room
### Outcome: Full accounts pipeline. WhatsApp presence. Workflow awareness.

**What Phase 3 delivers:**

Phase 3 is where Bridgit goes from a system your team uses to a team member your firm
works with — in your WhatsApp group, aware of who is working on what, and handling
the full accounts pipeline from source documents to filed returns.

**Full accounts pipeline:**
- VAT return prepared by Bridgit, ready for your review and sign-off
- Personal tax returns populated from the client's income sources
- Year-end accounts drafted with supporting schedules
- Making Tax Digital (MTD) submission pathway — tested and working
- All statutory submissions remain human-approved. Bridgit prepares, your accountant
  signs off

**WhatsApp and Meta integration:**
- Bridgit joins your WhatsApp group and sends a daily briefing: jobs due this week,
  what is waiting on a decision, what is ready for review
- Ask Bridgit in WhatsApp: "What's the status on ABC Ltd?" or "When is XYZ's VAT
  due?" — answered in the chat, no need to open a system
- Bridgit listens to the group, picks up mentions of clients and jobs, and updates the
  relevant records automatically
- Future: Bridgit in a client-facing WhatsApp group, answering basic client questions
  with MA's oversight

**Workflow awareness:**
- Bridgit knows who in the team is working on what job and at what stage
- When a Bridgit job completes, it links automatically to the WIP board — no double
  entry
- "Who's working on the ABC Ltd year-end?" — answered instantly in WhatsApp or the
  dashboard

**Specialist agents:**
- Phase 3 introduces specialist AI agents for VAT, personal tax, and accounts
  preparation, coordinated by Bridgit — like a senior coordinator routing work to
  the right junior and bringing results back for partner review

**Per-client portal:**
- A view for every client showing every job Bridgit has run, current year status,
  upcoming deadlines, and outstanding document requests
- The foundation for a client-facing login if MA Associates decides to offer that
  in the future

## What Stays the Same Throughout All Phases

- **A qualified accountant approves every output before it is used.** Bridgit does
  not file anything or represent anything to HMRC or Companies House without explicit
  human authorisation.
- **Nothing is guessed silently.** Every uncertain transaction either gets a confirmed
  coding with a logged reason, or comes back to your team with a specific question.
- **Full audit trail.** Every decision Bridgit makes is logged. Click any transaction
  and see exactly why it was coded that way.
- **Your data stays in your system.** Bridgit operates within the MA Associates
  database. AI providers used are business-tier with zero data retention — client data
  is not used to train any external model.

## Continuity and Protection

Three scenarios are written into the engagement:

1. **If the lead developer is unavailable:** MA Associates has full access to the
   code, hosting, and infrastructure. You can bring in another developer at any time
   without losing anything that has been built.
2. **If responsibility moves internally** (for example from Faraz to Qasim): a formal
   handover session is run with whoever takes over.
3. **Ongoing support:** a named developer from Armiza Labs remains the point of
   contact — not just one person with no backup.
